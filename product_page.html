Product Information:

Our Kiwi Cakes pancake mix is designed specifically for active individuals who want a quick, high-protein meal without sacrificing taste. Each pack contains 4–5 servings, with approximately 5 pancakes per serving. A scoop is included to ensure accurate portioning every time.

Preparation is simple and efficient: just add one scoop to a bowl, mix with water or milk, whisk until smooth, and cook. This makes Kiwi Cakes perfect for busy mornings, post-workout meals, or convenient snacks throughout the day.

Our Ingredients (per serving):

  • Oat Flour
  • Whey Protein Isolate
  • Egg White Powder
  • Buttermilk Powder
  • Coconut Sugar
  • Baking Powder
  • Pinch Sea Salt
  • Cinnamon

We prioritise transparency in our nutritional information to help customers make informed decisions. Clear labelling and allergy information are provided to ensure consumer safety.

Future Product Range

Although our main product is currently our high-protein pancake mix, Kiwi Cakes has the potential to expand into other fitness-focused products in the future. This could include protein shakes, protein bars, protein muffins, and other convenient snacks designed for people who want affordable and easy ways to increase their protein intake.

By developing a wider product range, Kiwi Cakes would be able to support different customer needs. For example, protein shakes could be useful for gym-goers who want a quick recovery drink after training, while protein bars could provide a convenient snack for students, athletes, and busy people who need protein on the go. This would help Kiwi Cakes become more than just a pancake mix brand, and instead become a simple, accessible nutrition brand for everyday New Zealanders.
